Our Story

Founded in 2018, Lumina Tech began with a simple mission: to eliminate the clutter in consumer electronics. We believe that technology should be a quiet companion that empowers you, not a loud distraction.

Based in Bangalore, our team of designers and engineers work tirelessly to source the finest materials and build products that last a lifetime. We aren't just selling gadgets; we're crafting the tools for your best work.
